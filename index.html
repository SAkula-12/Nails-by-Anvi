About Anvi

Nails By Anvi was created to provide beautiful, salon-quality nails at a more affordable price. With almost three years of experience and a lifelong passion for art, Anvi brings creativity and attention to detail to every set.

Anvi has been an artist since childhood and loves turning that creativity into wearable nail art. Gel-X is one of her favorite services, especially when creating intricate 3D gel designs.

Beautiful nails. Creative designs. A personal touch.
